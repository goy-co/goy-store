# Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** August 2026

---

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Goy Store project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Be respectful and constructive** in all interactions — assume good intent, and respond to ideas rather than people
- **Focus on technical merits** — critique the code, not the coder
- **Accept constructive criticism gracefully** — feedback is about the work, not a personal attack
- **Help others learn and grow** — share knowledge generously, mentor newcomers, and celebrate others' progress
- **Be inclusive** — use welcoming language, respect different perspectives, and actively seek to include underrepresented voices
- **Be patient** — not everyone has the same background or availability; allow space for different communication styles
- **Acknowledge contributions** — credit others for their work and ideas
- **Respect boundaries** — understand that not everyone wants to engage in the same way or at the same pace

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- **Harassment** — comments related to gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, disability, physical appearance, body size, age, or nationality that are unwelcome, hostile, or degrading
- **Discrimination** — exclusionary behavior based on personal identity or background
- **Trolling and insults** — deliberately inflammatory comments intended to provoke or demean
- **Personal attacks** — ad hominem arguments, name-calling, or character assassination
- **Public or private harassment** — following, stalking, photographing, or recording someone without consent; repeated unwanted contact
- **Doxxing** — publishing others' private information (name, address, workplace, email, phone) without explicit permission
- **Unwanted sexual attention** — sexualized comments, jokes, or imagery; inappropriate advances
- **Disruption** — sustained disruption of discussions, meetings, or community events
- **Retaliation** — punishing someone for reporting a violation or participating in an investigation
- **Plagiarism** — claiming others' work as your own, in code or in discussion

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Project documentation and wikis
- Community chat channels (Discord, Slack, Telegram, etc.)
- Project events (virtual or in-person)
- One-on-one communications related to the project
- Public spaces when representing the project (conferences, social media, etc.)

This Code of Conduct also applies to behavior outside the project when that behavior affects the safety or well-being of community members.

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it to:

📧 **legal@goycompany.com**

All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality. Reports should include:

- Your contact information (or anonymous if preferred)
- Names of those involved (or descriptions if names are unknown)
- Description of the behavior
- Date, time, and location/context
- Any supporting evidence (screenshots, links, etc.)
- Names of any witnesses

### Investigation

The Goy Company will acknowledge receipt of your report within **48 hours** and will investigate promptly. The investigation may include:

- Interviewing the reporter, the accused, and any witnesses
- Reviewing relevant communications or documentation
- Consulting with legal counsel if necessary

### Resolution

After investigation, The Goy Company will determine appropriate action, which may include:

| Severity | Possible Actions |
|---|---|
| **Minor** (first offense, unintentional) | Private warning, request for apology |
| **Moderate** (repeated minor, or single serious offense) | Temporary ban from project spaces, mandatory cooling-off period |
| **Severe** (harassment, threats, doxxing) | Permanent ban from all project spaces, public statement if warranted |

Factors considered in determining action:

- Severity and impact of the behavior
- Whether the behavior was intentional or repeated
- The accused's history in the community
- The accused's response to the report (acknowledgment, apology, willingness to change)

### Appeals

If you believe an enforcement decision was made in error, you may appeal by emailing legal@goycompany.com with a clear explanation of why you believe the decision should be reconsidered. Appeals will be reviewed by someone other than the original decision-maker.

### No Retaliation

The Goy Company strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a violation in good faith, participates in an investigation, or supports someone who reports. Retaliation is itself a violation of this Code of Conduct and will be treated as such.

## Responsibilities

### Community Members

- Read, understand, and follow this Code of Conduct
- Report violations when you see them
- Treat others with respect, even when you disagree
- Accept the consequences of your actions

### Maintainers and Leaders

- Model the behavior expected of all community members
- Enforce this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently
- Protect reporters from retaliation
- Maintain confidentiality of reports
- Communicate enforcement decisions transparently when appropriate
